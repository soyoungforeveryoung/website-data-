CARE

Lather, then hang it by the rope to dry. No soap dish needed.

It lasts longer if you hang it where the shower spray does not reach it directly.
When the bar is gone, the rope returns to the earth.

FAQ

Does it make a good gift?

It comes on a rope, ready to hang, and each bar is individually wrapped — a frequent choice as a gift.

Can I use it on my face?

It is made as a body cleanser. If you do use it on your face, keep it away from the eyes.

How long does it last?

With daily use, about two to three months. Hanging it to dry makes it last longer.
