Kunene, Namibia. There is a tree that grows only there.
The Himba call its resin omumbiri.

In the desert the sun stays overhead all day.
The Himba have worn omumbiri on their skin beneath it.
The written record alone goes back more than four hundred years.

INGREDIENT STORY

The omumbiri in this bar comes from that desert. We receive it directly from the Himba community trust, steam-distilled from the resin into an oil.

Sweet almond oil is blended in alongside it.

PRODUCTION

A Korean soap master shapes each bar by hand and air-dries it for a month.

FAQ

Does it make a good gift?

It comes on a rope, ready to hang, and each bar is individually wrapped — a frequent choice as a gift.

I have a nut allergy — can I use it?

This bar contains sweet almond (nut) oil. Please avoid it if you have a nut allergy.

How long does it last?

With daily use, about two to three months. Hanging it to dry makes it last longer.
