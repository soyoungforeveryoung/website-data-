CARE

First burn

Let it burn about three hours, until the surface has melted edge to edge.

Before each use

Trim the wick to 3–5 mm.

Each burn

Keep it under three hours.

Burn away from draughts. Never leave a burning candle unattended, and keep it out of reach of children and pets.

FAQ

Does it make a good gift?

It comes with a ceramic lid, and the vessel can be washed and reused once the candle is finished — it is one of our most requested gifts.

Which room suits it best?

The scent holds best in a room you can close — a bedroom, a study, a bathroom.

What can I do with the empty vessel?

Remove the remaining wax and wash it. It works as a small vase or a holder for odds and ends.

How long does it last?

Burning three hours at a time, about 60 hours — roughly twenty uses.
